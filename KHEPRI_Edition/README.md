# KHEPRI: GALAXY EXPLORERS - Egypt x NASA (Kids Edition)

Open this folder in **Godot 4.7** (tested on 4.7 stable, standard build, no C#) and press **F5**.
No plugins needed.

A friendly, funny 2D space-adventure for **junior (ages 8-10)** and **middle school (ages 11-14)** explorers,
inspired by the cozy planet-hopping feel of Little Known Galaxy. Hop between silly planets, keep your ship happy,
talk to Egyptian legends on the radio, solve Puzzle Gates and bring your science home to Earth.

## How it plays
- **Pick your explorer level** on the title screen: *Junior Explorer* (gentler, easier questions) or
  *Middle School Scientist* (more supplies pressure, harder questions).
- **Choose a route**: Earth > Moon, Moon > Mars, Earth > Mars (direct) or Moon and Back.
- **Galaxy map**: pick a glowing planet to visit next. Every planet has a silly name.
  - Surprise! = a random ship event (choose how to react)
  - **Puzzle Gate** = a friendly space critter asks a science question
  - Science Planet = scan it with real instruments and learn what the data means
  - Friendly Shop = refuel, restock, or trade science for spare parts
  - Quiet Space = a breather
- **Ship**: share the reactor power between rooms, move crew, grow food in the Bio Bay, and keep the 7 bars
  (Power, Oxygen, Water, Food, Fuel, Hull, Morale) healthy.
- **Tractor Beam** (old "Weapons") powers a free hint at Puzzle Gates when it has 2+ power. **Hint Crystals**
  remove a wrong answer.
- **Radio**: Egyptian legends and funny NASA offices call with advice and silly choices.
- **Care packages**: if a supply runs out, Mission Control sends help (3 per mission) instead of ending your game.
  If they run out the mission ends gently and you can try again.
- **Science report** at the end: stars, rank, what you learned and a next question to explore.

## What's educational
- 32 quiz questions (16 junior, 16 more for middle school) with a kind explanation after every answer.
- Real instruments, scan data with units and quality, a knowledge library, mission timeline and signal delay.
- All teaching text is in `data/education.json` and `scripts/game_data.gd` (QUIZ) - add more freely.
